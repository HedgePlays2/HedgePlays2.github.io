THE PEOPLE & PROJECTS BEHIND THE GAMES

Credits

A game-by-game credit list for the titles currently featured in Hedge Web Arcade. It separates the original game creators from web-port authors and from Hedge, which hosts or maintains some entries. Credits are provided for attribution; hosting a game does not mean Hedge created, owns, or has special affiliation with it.

Attribution note: Where a port author or individual contributor could not be reliably confirmed from the information available, this page says so instead of guessing. If you are a creator or rights holder and would like a credit corrected or expanded, please use the arcade's GitHub issue page.
